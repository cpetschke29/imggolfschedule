New week

Brings over AM, PM and Camp with their coaches and groups, plus the day themes. A Holiday or Summer camp week replaces AM and PM with one camp schedule and a Breakthroughs tab. News, PTO, weekend notes, coverage, tee time requests, campers and Breakthroughs are not copied. The new week starts unpublished.

Names on the first screen

One name per line. Coaches pick their name from this list to see their whole day. Only you can change it, from Edit mode.

Assign zones

Fills in the zones for this week's Practice and Cross sport events (AM and PM). It keeps pods from sharing a zone at the same time, rotates your sets through the week, spreads zones over the semester, and gives each pod Functional once a week.

Paste the hourly schedule

In Excel, select the schedule (the "Week of" rows, names and hours), copy, and paste here. Weeks that match a week in the planner are filled in, and their current hours are replaced.

Paste campers

In Excel, select the camper rows (include the header row if you like), copy, and paste here. If a row holds only a coach's name, the campers under it go to that coach.
