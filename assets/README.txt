WEEK PLANNER
============

To open it: double-click index.html (it opens in your browser).
Coach view link:  index.html#coach   (add =Larry to open one coach's schedule)

FOLDERS
  index.html  - the page itself (top level)
  css/     styles.css  - all colors, spacing and layout
  js/      core.js         - schedule data, trip calculation, Excel export
           app.js          - screens, drag and drop, Analytics, News & PTO
           sample-data.js  - the sample week built from your Fall Master file
           vendor/         - two small open-source libraries (Excel read/write, drag and drop),
                             included so the planner works without an internet connection
  assets/  README.txt, DEMO_Fall_Master_2026.xlsx - the original file the sample week came from

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your schedule is saved in the browser you edit in. Use Export Excel to back it up or share it.
  - To change how it looks, edit css/styles.css. To change what it does, edit js/app.js.
  - Keep index.html next to the css, js and assets folders; it loads files with relative paths.
  - To put this on an internal site for your coaches, contact IMG Academy support.
  - The Analytics PIN is a simple lock, not strong security.
